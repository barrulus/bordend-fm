Nordend · 4609 m

A native file manager for Wayland: fast, light and safe.

No GTK, no Qt, no GLib: C++20 straight on Wayland and OpenGL ES, with its own renderer for text, icons, scrolling and animation. At home on any Wayland desktop, and right at home on Noctalia.

Install Read the docs Source on GitHub

Young software, used every day by its author. MIT licence.

Fast

  • ~65 ms to the first frame
  • 0.7 ms to show a 10 000-file folder
  • < 100 ms for a raw photo in Quick Look

Every figure is measured, with the tool to measure it again.

Light

  • 3.2 MB binary, 17 libraries
  • ~25 MB of memory of its own
  • 0 CPU while nothing moves

No toolkit underneath: Nordend talks to the compositor directly.

Safe

  • Files from outside read in a caged helper
  • seccomp and landlock, our own: no files, no network
  • A hostile file costs at most its preview

Nothing overwritten without asking, a real trash, and Ctrl+Z.

Its own readers

TIFF (LZW, Deflate, PackBits, JPEG, tiles, BigTIFF), PGM and PPM are read by code written for Nordend, checked pixel for pixel against libtiff and fuzzed. PNG, JPEG and GIF go through wuffs, memory-safe by construction. Raw files show the JPEG their camera stored: the camera's own rendering, exposure and colour.

Three views

Columns, a thumbnail grid and a details table (Ctrl+1 2 3); switching keeps your place and your selection.

Quick Look

Space fills the window with the photo, raw files included, or the text; the arrows walk the folder.

Command palette

Ctrl+K: every command, place and tab, fuzzy-matched, the recently used first.

Find files

Ctrl+F: every file under the folder, first results in milliseconds even among 270 000.

Tabs

Ctrl+T or a middle click; each tab keeps its folder, view and history, all back at the next start.

Careful with your files

Copy and move with conflict questions, darktable sidecars that follow their photo, the freedesktop trash, undo and redo.

At home on any desktop, right at home on Noctalia

Nordend takes your desktop's light or dark style and accent colour on GNOME and Plasma; on Noctalia it wears your Noctalia theme, live, and its icon follows your accent in the dock.
